TEAM OPERATIONS · TRAINING 01

Confident, careful directory administration.

A practical video-training guide for the CGG Luxury Directory admin team. Learn the live-only portal, review business listings responsibly, inspect website analytics, and protect member trust.

Suggested runtime: 7–9 minutesFor authorized administratorsGreen · Gold · CGG™
Important: This is the video script and self-guided training companion—not a recorded video. The portal is still being wired up in places. Only use controls that are visibly connected and verified. Never assume a placeholder screen is a working production feature.
Training checklist0 of 8 complete
Tick each topic as you complete the training.

Video storyboard & narration

Each card includes what the viewer sees and what the presenter says.

01

Welcome to CGG Admin Studio

0:00–0:40

ON SCREEN

Show the CGG™ logo, the forest-green and gold interface, and the Admin Studio sign-in page.

NARRATION

“Welcome to CGG Admin Studio. Our administrator team helps keep the directory accurate, consistent and useful. Access is reserved for authorized team members. Sign in only with your approved administrator account.”

KEY POINT

Never share sign-in links, passwords, or verification codes with another person.

02

Live-only access and connection checks

0:40–1:40

ON SCREEN

Show the secure sign-in screen, then the LIVE · SECURE label and Refresh control after authorization.

NARRATION

“CGG Admin Studio is now live-only. There is no demo mode or sample-member workspace. Sign in with the authorized administrator account, confirm the live connection, and refresh records if something looks out of date. If the connection fails, stop and report the error rather than relying on a guessed number.”

KEY POINT

Only use figures returned from the connected database. Never invent or estimate missing metrics.

03

Understand live overview and analytics

1:40–2:20

ON SCREEN

Show the overview, then point out the connection status and any clearly labeled live-data snapshot.

NARRATION

“The overview shows connected database counts. Website Analytics charts show stored listing-view counters and live directory records. These are not full visitor/session analytics; Google Analytics 4 is not yet connected.”

KEY POINT

Never report a missing metric as zero unless the live data confirms zero.

04

Find and review listings

2:20–3:35

ON SCREEN

Open Directory Listings. Search by business name, city or slug. Select a record and inspect its fields before making any change.

NARRATION

“Use search to find the exact business. Confirm its name, location and current status before editing. Keep descriptions factual and concise. Check website and booking links carefully. Save only when the change is supported and you understand it.”

KEY POINT

Never invent a listing fact, change a tier without authorization, or edit a similar-looking business by mistake.

05

Subscribers and privacy

3:35–4:25

ON SCREEN

Open Traveler Subscribers. Show the search and marketing-consent filters; use fictional or masked information in any recording.

NARRATION

“Subscriber information is private. Look up a record only for a legitimate admin task. Marketing consent must be respected and interpreted from the stored record. Do not copy member details into personal notes, public messages or an unapproved spreadsheet.”

KEY POINT

Do not expose personal email addresses or consent timestamps in public training recordings.

06

Claims, submissions and reviews

4:25–5:25

ON SCREEN

Open Reviews & Moderation to see live traveler reviews, chat reports, claims and listing submissions. Show the status controls without changing a real record during training.

NARRATION

“Review each request against the directory’s standards. Check evidence and duplicates. Approve or reject only after verification. Use the report status controls to mark a report reviewing, resolved or dismissed. If unsure or there is a conflict of interest, pause and escalate.”

KEY POINT

Approvals and rejections may affect real businesses. Never test these actions on a real request.

07

Safe changes and escalation

5:25–6:35

ON SCREEN

Show the listing edit form, the live Audit Log filters and the Export CSV control. Also show Settings & Access and the Admin Guide link. Do not reveal access tokens, secret keys or account recovery links.

NARRATION

“Before saving, verify every field. Make one focused change at a time. After saving, refresh and confirm the result. The audit log records available directory changes; it is not a full audit of every action. If data looks wrong, stop and notify the lead administrator.”

KEY POINT

Never put service-role keys or other secrets into the browser, chat, screenshots or training video.

08

Wrap-up and team standards

6:35–7:15

ON SCREEN

Show the training checklist and the return-to-portal link.

NARRATION

“Our team approach creates a second set of eyes, more consistent reviews, clearer accountability and a better experience for members. Use the right mode, verify before saving, protect member privacy and escalate uncertain decisions. Trust is built one careful update at a time.”

KEY POINT

When in doubt: pause, preserve the record, and ask the lead administrator.
