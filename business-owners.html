Our curation approach

What we’re looking for

  • Independent or locally operated boutique hotels in Colombia.
  • Distinctive architecture, interiors, atmosphere, or a strong connection to the destination.
  • Consistent guest care and clear, current property information.
  • Official website or another reliable way for travelers to contact the property.
  • Permission to use any submitted photos and copy, plus a process to correct details when they change.

Elegance and character matter more than a rigid star-rating cutoff. Every property will be reviewed before being presented as verified or as a formal partner.

Planned process

Simple, transparent, and owner-led.

Step 1
Share property details

When applications open, owners will be able to provide official links, contact information, photos, and a description.

Step 2
Review and confirm

Our team will check core details and confirm permission to publish the property’s content and links.

Step 3
Publish and improve

Approved profiles can be updated as amenities, images, and contact details change. Paid options will be clearly disclosed.
