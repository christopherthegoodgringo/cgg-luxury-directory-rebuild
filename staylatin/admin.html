Quality & trust

Review rules

Recommended safeguards for the future live workflow.

Before a listing is approved

  • Confirm the submitter is the owner or an authorized representative.
  • Check the official website, contact details and physical location.
  • Review submitted photos and confirm permission to publish them.
  • Check for duplicate listings and misleading claims.
  • Use a separate verification status; approval alone does not prove independent verification.
  • Record who reviewed the listing, the decision, timestamp and reason.
  • Require authenticated administrator access and role-based permissions for every write action.
  • Keep traveler accounts and business-owner accounts logically separate.
  • Never expose service-role keys, payment secrets or private business submissions in public frontend code.
