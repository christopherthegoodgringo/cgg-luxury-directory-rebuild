For independent property owners

Four ways to be part of StayLatin.

Proposed launch pricing for business memberships. These plans are separate from the public property references above.

01 · Start here

Basic

Free

Standard business profile, core property details, Expedia and Trip.com booking links when a matching property profile can be verified, listing corrections and eligibility for review.

02 · Trust & detail

Verified

$9.99 / month

Everything in Basic, up to 5 approved photos, owner/property review and correction support. Expedia and Trip.com booking links when matching property profiles are verifiable. Verification badge only after passing review.

03 · More visibility

Featured

$19.99 / month

Basic benefits plus featured styling, enhanced eligible placement, up to 5 approved photos and performance reporting when available. Expedia and Trip.com booking links remain available when matching property profiles are verifiable.

04 · Maximum presence

Premium

$39.99 / month

Premium styling, highest eligible placement, expanded profile copy, priority listing-update support and a direct link to the property’s official booking website. Premium uses the direct-booking link instead of Expedia and Trip.com buttons.

Recommended launch prices; recurring billing is not connected and no payments are collected. Expedia and Trip.com links are shown only when the property has a verifiable profile on that platform.
