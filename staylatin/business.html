Business profiles

Choose how you grow.

Recommended launch pricing. Plans are not yet purchasable and billing is inactive.

Basic

Free

• Public business profile with name, location and stay type
• Core property details and official website
• Expedia and Trip.com links only when matching property profiles are verifiable
• Submit corrections and request a listing review
• Basic discovery in the directory
• Access to business-owner community chat after submitting a property

Verified

$9.99/mo

Everything in Basic, plus:
• Up to 5 approved property photos
• Ownership and property-detail review
• Verified badge only after passing review
• Help correcting profile details
• Stronger trust signals for prospective guests
• Checklist for completing your listing
• Early access to owner resources as they launch

Featured

$19.99/mo

Everything in Verified, plus:
• Featured profile styling and eligible placement
• Up to 5 approved photos with curated presentation
• Expanded property highlights and amenities
• Seasonal offers and special-note fields when released
• Performance reporting when available
• Priority correction queue over lower tiers
• Improved visibility opportunities in relevant searches
• Expedia and Trip.com links when matching profiles are verifiable

Premium

$39.99/mo

Everything in Featured, plus:
• Premium profile presentation and highest eligible placement
• Expanded story, room highlights and property information
• Direct link to the property's official booking website instead of Expedia/Trip.com buttons
• Priority profile-update support
• Enhanced offer and seasonal-highlight placement when released
• Advanced performance insights when available
• Early access to new business tools
• Enhanced owner resources and onboarding support

Prices are proposed for launch, not active subscriptions. Expedia and Trip.com links require a verifiable matching property profile. Premium uses the property’s direct official booking link instead. Verification is earned through review, not purchased. No payment is taken on this page, and placement or bookings are not guaranteed.
