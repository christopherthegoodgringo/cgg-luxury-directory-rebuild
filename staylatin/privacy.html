Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Applies to StayLatin websites and account tools.

StayLatin (“we,” “us”) helps travelers discover independent stays across Latin America. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details you provide when joining or signing in (such as name, email, password, and account type: traveler, business, agent, or admin).
  • Profile and listing content you submit (saved favorites, property details, photos you upload, and messages you send through our forms).
  • Technical data such as browser type, device, approximate location derived from IP, pages viewed, and cookies or similar storage used for sign-in and preferences (including language).
  • Communications when you email us or request a password reset.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, and show the right workspace for your role.
  • To operate the directory, favorites, and related traveler or business tools.
  • To improve the site, fix bugs, and understand how listings are discovered.
  • To send essential service messages (password resets, security notices). Marketing email is optional and only with your consent where required.

3. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on where you live, we process data to perform our contract with you, with your consent, for legitimate interests in running a safe directory, or to meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers that host our site, authentication, or email (for example cloud database and hosting partners), under agreements that limit use to our instructions.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect users, StayLatin, or others from harm or fraud.
  • Business transfers if StayLatin is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice when required.

Public directory listings and content you choose to publish may be visible to anyone visiting the site.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use necessary cookies/local storage for login sessions, saved favorites on your device, and language preference. You can clear these in your browser; some features may stop working until you sign in again.

6. Retention

We keep account and listing data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance, then delete or anonymize it when no longer needed.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (encrypted connections, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

StayLatin is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. International transfers

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our providers operate. We take steps to protect it in line with this policy.

11. Third-party links

Listings may link to Airbnb, Expedia, Trip.com, Hostelworld, or property websites. Their privacy practices are their own; review their policies before sharing data there.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new date here and, for material changes, provide additional notice when appropriate.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact options on StayLatin or email the site operator listed on your account materials.
