TRUST & QUALITY

Reviews & moderation

Review reported content, member submissions and corrections.

Moderation queue

Priority queues for reported traveler chat, reviews, directory claims and listing submissions. Planned actions include review, resolve, dismiss, hide content, add internal notes and preserve an audit trail.

COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Messages & reports

Support workflows for traveler accounts and community safety.

Support inbox

Centralized reports, account requests, message safety flags, status tracking, internal notes and resolution history.

MEMBERSHIP OPERATIONS

Membership & plans

Review access tiers and prepare for future membership billing.

Launch-phase membership controls

Designed to distinguish free launch access from any future monthly membership fee. No billing provider is activated by this preview. Plan changes, payment history, cancellations and revenue reporting must be connected to verified backend records before use.

CONTENT QUALITY

Content & corrections

Keep member and directory information accurate, consistent and trustworthy.

Corrections workbench

Proposed workflow: queue incoming correction requests, compare current and proposed values, approve or reject edits, attach notes and record who changed what and when. Changes should never overwrite the audit history.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Audit log

A traceable record of administrative actions and corrections.

Change history

Planned filters for administrator, date, action and record type; before-and-after values; exportable change history; and immutable audit events. Live writes should record the authenticated admin identity.

SECURITY & CONFIGURATION

Settings & access

Manage administrator access, roles and operational preferences.

Secure administrator access

Live portal should enforce administrator privileges on the server and database, not just hide pages in the browser. Never place a Supabase service-role key in this static website. Admin roles, MFA policy, session revocation and permission audit are part of the production wiring stage.
